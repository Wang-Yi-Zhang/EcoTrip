## 一、給 Tabin 報價業者（當地業者 A）

**Subject: Tabin 5D4N private tour – clarification before booking (29 Jan – 6 Feb 2027, 4 pax)**

Dear [name],

Thank you for the quotation for the 5 Days 4 Nights Tabin private tour. Before we confirm, could you please clarify the following?

1. **Package basis.** Is the quote "standard package + private add-ons" or "a private itinerary replacing the standard package"? Does the RM5,100 per person base package already include:
   - scheduled return transfers Lahad Datu ↔ Tabin,
   - guided walks (bird walk, Lipad waterfall, mud volcano),
   - the dusk drive and night safari,
   - the overnight at the Lipad mud volcano tower?

   We want to avoid paying twice for the same service.
2. **Dates.** We plan to check in on **30 Jan 2027** and check out on **3 Feb 2027** (4 nights).
3. **Private Return Transfer.** What are the pick-up and drop-off points (Lahad Datu airport or town)? On **3 Feb** we continue to Danum Valley the same day. Can we leave Tabin early (around 07:00) to reach Lahad Datu before 09:30?
4. **Private guide and private vehicle.** Each is quoted for 4 days. Which 4 days do they cover?
5. **Private night safari.** It is quoted for 3 nights. Which 3 nights? Can we add a 4th night, and at what price?
6. **Validity.** Is the quote valid for Jan–Feb 2027, including the Chinese New Year period? What are the deposit, payment and cancellation terms?
7. **Occupancy.** What is the price for single occupancy or an odd number of travellers?
8. **Lipad tower.** Will the Lipad mud volcano observation tower be open in Jan–Feb 2027?
9. **Rules and fees.** Are there rules on lighting or photography during night activities? Is there a camera fee, tourism tax or conservation fee not included in the quote?

Best regards,
[your name]
